## Results

Fourteen RCTs enrolling approximately 960 participants were included. FMT demonstrated superior efficacy over antibiotic comparators in the majority of trials: cure rates ranged from 71–90% for FMT versus 19–52% for vancomycin, fidaxomicin, or 12-strain bacteriotherapy (Cammarota et al. 2015; Hvas et al. 2019; Rode et al. 2021). A placebo-controlled trial confirmed FMT efficacy in early CDI (90% vs 33%; Baunwall et al. 2022). Donor FMT by colonoscopy outperformed autologous FMT (90.9% vs 62.5%; Kelly et al. 2016), while one trial of single-enema FMT was stopped for futility versus a vancomycin taper (43.8% vs 58.3%; Hota et al. 2017). Head-to-head comparisons showed equivalence between oral capsule and colonoscopy delivery (96.2% both arms; Kao et al. 2017) and between frozen and fresh preparations (Lee et al. 2016). Multiple FMT infusions were superior to single infusion in severe refractory CDI (100% vs 75%; Ianiro et al. 2018). Adjunctive bezlotoxumab offered no additional benefit in IBD-associated rCDI (Allegretti et al. 2024). Substantial heterogeneity was observed across trials in recurrence thresholds for eligibility, FMT preparation, delivery route, comparator, and follow-up duration.

## Methods

A systematic search of PubMed was conducted to identify randomized controlled trials of fecal microbiota transplantation for *Clostridioides difficile* infection. A total of 220 records were identified; 219 were retrieved for screening after applying retrieval limits. Following removal of 2 duplicates, 217 unique records were screened at the title and abstract level. Thirteen records were confirmed for inclusion and 201 excluded at this stage. Three records with uncertain eligibility were escalated to full-text review; one was subsequently included and two excluded. A total of 14 RCTs were included in the final synthesis. Studies were eligible if they were randomized trials with at least one FMT arm reporting clinical outcomes for CDI; economic evaluations, recruitment-only reports, and non-randomized designs were excluded.
